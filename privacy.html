Privacy Policy for ThunderNet

Last updated: August 16, 2026

ThunderNet is a networking education and utility app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

ThunderNet does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require you to create an account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data brokers.

Network Tools

Some ThunderNet features inspect information available on your device or local network, such as network status, IP addresses, DNS responses, Wi-Fi details, and results of diagnostics you initiate. This information is used only on your device to provide the requested feature. It is not sent to us or stored on our servers.

Purchases and Subscriptions

ThunderNet offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions. Payments and purchase records are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

ThunderNet does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Security

Because ThunderNet does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain a server-side database of user data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to ThunderNet or applicable law. The current version will always be available at this page, with its effective date shown above.

Contact
